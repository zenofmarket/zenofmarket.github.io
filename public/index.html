The Dow rose 0.83%, the S&P 500 gained 0.59%, and the Nasdaq added 0.51%. Software, Amazon, healthcare and tower companies led. Chips, Apple and wireless carriers lagged, so Friday repaired the indexes without restoring the old leadership.

JOINT READTHE REBOUND CAME FROM ROTATION, NOT MACRO RELIEF.

Yields and oil stayed high. Stocks rose because buyers moved into different groups and reacted to company news.

02

MARKET ROTATION

SOFTWARE LED. CHIPS AND TELECOM LAGGED.

The heatmap shows a broad rebound with a clear split inside technology. Microsoft, Amazon, software, financials and healthcare rose. Apple and several chipmakers fell, while wireless carriers took the day's sharpest losses.

ROTATION CLASSIFICATIONBROAD REBOUND WITH SOFTWARE-LED TECH

Capital returned to equities, but not to the same places that led earlier in the week. Software and company-specific winners carried more of the move than AI hardware.

LEADERS

SOFTWARE AND AMAZON REBOUNDED

Microsoft rose 2.38%, Amazon gained 3.29%, Palantir added 5.17% and Oracle climbed 4.21%.

PARTICIPATION

FINANCIALS AND HEALTHCARE JOINED

Banks, payment companies and drugmakers added support. Humana rose 11.6% after encouraging Medicare plan ratings.

LAGGARDS

CHIPS, APPLE AND CARRIERS FELL

Apple lost 1.11%, AMD fell 2.03% and Intel dropped 2.22%. T-Mobile, AT&T and Verizon fell sharply.

03

MARKET DRIVERS

BUYERS RETURNED, BUT COMPANY NEWS SET THE MAP.

Thursday's AI selloff created room for a rebound. The money went first to software and cloud names. A SpaceX spectrum purchase and new Medicare ratings then produced much larger moves in telecom, towers and health insurers.
