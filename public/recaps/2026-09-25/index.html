SEP 25, 2026 · MOVE AT 104 · LEVERAGE WATCH

THE RALLY BROADENED.THE RISK MOVED UNDERNEATH.

September 24 showed that diplomacy could rescue the index. September 25 showed what happened when that relief lasted longer. Softer language from Iran and reports of more detailed talks pushed oil lower, eased some inflation pressure and helped eight of eleven S&P sectors finish higher. The S&P 500 gained 0.51%, the Dow rose 0.93% and the Nasdaq Composite added 0.42%. Yet the 10-year yield remained above 5.2% and Treasury volatility had jumped sharply. The rally was real, but the next warning was no longer a round yield number. It was whether bond stress reached credit, banks and leveraged positions.

MARKET ROTATION

MONEY MOVED WIDER. TECH STILL SPLIT.

Microsoft gained 3.66%, Apple rose 1.53%, Dell added 5.01% and semiconductor equipment names advanced. The rebound also reached JPMorgan, Bank of America, GE, Caterpillar and Costco. That was healthier than the narrow rescue a day earlier. Still, Meta fell 3.33%, Intel lost 3.45% and energy weakened with oil. 03

MARKET DRIVERS

THE MARKET FOUND RELIEF. RISK MOVED TO THE PLUMBING.

Diplomatic progress lowered oil and gave stocks room to recover. The more important question sat below the index. Treasury volatility had risen from roughly 78 to 104 in two sessions, even as VIX stayed near 15 and credit remained calm. That combination says pressure is building in bonds, but it has not yet become a broad financial break.
