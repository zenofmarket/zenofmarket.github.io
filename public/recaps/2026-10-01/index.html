Both Treasury maturities and crude ended above their September 1 levels. The October 1 retreat helped equities, but the three markets did not yet deliver synchronized easing.

02

MARKET ROTATION

AI HELD THE INDEX. THE REST STAYED SELECTIVE.

Memory, semiconductor equipment, software and power-infrastructure names carried the strongest pockets of the session. Internet platforms, healthcare and banks remained weak. The rebound had enough leadership to defend the index, but not enough breadth to signal a clean risk-on turn.

ROTATION CLASSIFICATIONGROWTH-LED REPAIR WITH HIGH DISPERSION

The index recovered, but capital did not move evenly. AI-linked winners rallied while several large platform, healthcare and financial names remained under pressure.

LEADERS

MEMORY + AI INFRASTRUCTURE

Micron gained 3.03%, while Nvidia, AMD and semiconductor-equipment names held the AI complex together.

PARTICIPATION

SOFTWARE + INDUSTRIAL EQUIPMENT

Salesforce, Synopsys, Caterpillar and GE Vernova added a second layer of support beyond the chip group.

LAGGARDS

INTERNET + HEALTHCARE + BANKS

Alphabet, Netflix, Johnson & Johnson, Amgen, Thermo Fisher, Citigroup and Bank of America remained weak.

03

THREE FORCES TURNED A BREAKDOWN INTO A RECOVERY.

The long end eased, the S&P reclaimed closing-basis support, and AI-linked demand absorbed pressure inside the growth complex. The combination stabilized the tape without resolving the broader rate problem.
