OCT 8, 2026 · AI HARDWARE SELLOFF · BROADER ROTATION · BOND RELIEF

AI SOLD OFF.THE REST OF THE MARKET HELD.

The Nasdaq fell 1.39% after a new report changed how investors viewed OpenAI's revenue. Nvidia, Broadcom, AMD and Micron fell sharply. But the Dow edged higher, most S&P 500 stocks rose, and lower long yields helped the rest of the market hold.

MARKET READ

THIS WAS ROTATION, NOT A FULL MARKET BREAK

The Dow rose and most S&P 500 stocks gained even as the Nasdaq fell sharply.

01

MACRO CONFIRMATION

THE AUCTION LOWERED YIELDS. OIL KEPT THE RELIEF IN CHECK.

The 10-year yield fell to 5.22%. A $22 billion 30-year auction helped pull the long bond from 5.73% during the day to 5.60% at the close. WTI crude jumped to $91.49, so the inflation risk did not disappear.

JOINT READBONDS HELPED. OIL LIMITED THE RELIEF.

Lower yields supported the broader market. The jump in crude kept inflation pressure in view and stopped the session from becoming a clean rate-relief trade.

02

MARKET ROTATION

CHIPS FELL. ENERGY, BANKS AND DEFENSIVES TOOK THE OTHER SIDE.

The heatmap shows a sharp split. AI hardware and cloud names sold off, while oil producers, banks, payment companies and consumer staples gained. Apple also rose. The weakness was concentrated rather than market-wide.

ROTATION CLASSIFICATIONAI HARDWARE DE-RATING WITH BROADER ROTATION

Money left the most crowded AI names, but it did not leave the market. It moved into groups with current earnings, cash flow and defensive demand.

LEADERS

ENERGY AND STAPLES LED

Exxon rose 2.71%, Chevron gained 3.12%, Walmart added 2.22% and Pepsi climbed 2.27%.

PARTICIPATION

THE BROADER MARKET HELD

Apple gained 1.11%, banks and payment companies rose, and about two-thirds of S&P 500 stocks finished higher.

LAGGARDS

AI HARDWARE TOOK THE HIT

Nvidia fell 2.94%, Broadcom lost 4.35%, AMD dropped 3.90% and Micron declined 4.79%.
