SEP 11, 2026 · OIL RETREATS · STOCKS DEFY THE SCRIPT

CPI WAS HAWKISH.STOCKS RALLIED ANYWAY.

Core consumer prices rose 0.3% in August and expectations for a near-term Fed hike strengthened, yet every major index gained close to 1%. The contradiction shifts attention from the number itself to what traders had already hedged, what falling oil released and what the bond market still refused to surrender. Relief was real and breadth was broad. The 10-year near 5% remains the test of whether the rally can become conviction.

01

MACRO CONFIRMATION

THE RALLY FOUGHT A TIGHTER MONTHLY BACKDROP.

Over the full month ending September 11, the 10-year yield moved from 4.70% to 4.96%, the 30-year moved from 5.24% to 5.35%, and front-month WTI moved from $83.20 to $100.05. The 10-year gained 26 basis points and crude 20.3%, so the equity rebound came against, not with, the monthly macro trend.

02

MARKET ROTATION

THE RALLY WAS BROAD. HARDWARE LED.

Green spread across communication services, finance, consumer shares and industrials. Dell surged 11.97%, HPE 12.46%, Arista 5.61% and Cisco 4.37%, while Oracle fell 1.74%. The map confirms genuine participation, but it also shows that investors rewarded financing quality and infrastructure exposure rather than every AI headline.

LEADERSHIP

DELL + HPE

Dell gained 11.97% and HPE 12.46%, with Arista and Cisco joining the move. AI spending translated into a wider infrastructure and networking bid.

PARTICIPATION

MEGA-CAPS + FINANCE

Amazon rose 1.94%, Alphabet 1.77% and Apple 1.75%. Banks, capital markets and many industrial names also advanced, giving the rebound real breadth.

LAGGARDS

ORACLE + STORAGE

Oracle lost 1.74% after an early jump faded, while SanDisk and Seagate fell 3.50% and 3.73%. Nvidia and Micron were roughly flat despite the broader rally.

03

MARKET DRIVERS

THE NUMBER DID NOT LIFT STOCKS. THE POSITIONING RELEASE DID.

Core CPI remained firm and hike expectations strengthened, but Brent crude fell 2.8% and VIX dropped toward 15 to 16. The cleanest explanation is mechanical: oil reduced the inflation premium, event hedges came off and short covering carried the rebound before fresh conviction had to arrive.
