SEP 21, 2026 · DE-ESCALATION HOPES · AI LEADS

THE SUMMIT GAVETHE MARKET A PATH.

The conflict was not over. Attention turned to the Trump-Xi summit and the possibility that talks on trade and AI could open a wider diplomatic path. Some investors saw a possible role for Beijing in easing U.S.-Iran tensions. Nothing was confirmed, but the idea lowered the perceived risk of a deeper oil shock. Crude and Treasury yields fell, then money rushed back into AI. The S&P 500 gained 1.49%, the Dow rose 0.71% and the Nasdaq Composite jumped 2.26% to a record close.

MACRO CONFIRMATION

SUMMIT RELIEF ARRIVED AFTER A MONTH OF TIGHTENING.

Over the full month ending September 21, the 10-year yield moved from 4.74% to 4.96%, the 30-year moved from 5.27% to 5.29%, and front-month WTI moved from $87.06 to $95.78. The long end was nearly unchanged, but the 10-year and crude still finished above their monthly starting points.

RELIEF TURNED INTO AN AI RUSH.

Intel gained 12.14%, AMD 9.95%, Marvell 5.38% and Nvidia 2.30%. Meta surged 11.34%, while Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon and Tesla also rose. Energy moved the other way because the same drop in oil that helped the wider market hurt producers.

03

MARKET DRIVERS

ONE DIPLOMATIC HOPE SET OFF THE RALLY.

The Trump-Xi summit was already a major event for trade, tariffs, AI and critical minerals. The market also saw a chance that Beijing could help create a path toward easing U.S.-Iran tensions. That outcome was not confirmed, but the possibility lowered the oil risk premium. AI and post-expiration flows then made the move larger.
