SEP 10, 2026 · 10Y NEARS 5% · CPI HOLDS THE FORK

THE SELLOFF STAYED ORDERLY.CPI HOLDS THE RED BUTTON.

All three major indexes fell as hotter producer inflation, rising oil and a 25-basis-point ECB hike pushed the rate threat back to center stage. The harder question is whether bonds are simply repricing inflation or testing how much tightening policymakers can tolerate before something breaks. There is no evidence of an organized campaign. The next CPI print, the 10-year near 5% and VIX below 20 will reveal whether this is pressure, relief or forced liquidation.

MACRO CONFIRMATION

PPI HIT A TAPE ALREADY TIGHTENED BY OIL.

Over the full month ending September 10, the 10-year yield moved from 4.72% to 4.95%, the 30-year moved from 5.25% to 5.37%, and front-month WTI moved from $82.13 to $102.48. The 10-year rose 23 basis points, the 30-year 12, and crude 24.8%, giving the PPI shock broad cross-asset confirmation.

02

MARKET ROTATION

CHIPS TOOK THE HIT. APPLE CARRIED THE EXCEPTION.

The heatmap shows broad pressure across semiconductors, computer hardware and industrials. Nvidia fell 2.37%, Micron 4.90%, Intel 5.57% and Oracle 5.38%, while Apple gained 3.56%. This was a rate-sensitive rotation out of recent winners, not indiscriminate liquidation.

ROTATION CLASSIFICATIONCAPITAL LEFT THE CROWDED WINNERS, BUT IT DID NOT LEAVE THE MARKET BLINDLY.

The heatmap shows broad pressure across semiconductors, computer hardware and industrials. Nvidia fell 2.37%, Micron 4.90%, Intel 5.57% and Oracle 5.38%, while Apple gained 3.56%. This was a rate-sensitive rotation out of recent winners, not indiscriminate liquidation.

LEADERSHIP

APPLE + DEFENSIVE POCKETS

Apple gained 3.56% against a falling market. Tobacco, selected healthcare names and regional banks also attracted buyers, preserving clear places to hide.

PARTICIPATION

GOOGLE + ENERGY HELD

Alphabet gained 0.59%, Microsoft edged higher and Exxon rose 0.61%. Participation was narrow, but it was wide enough to reject a full-system risk exit.

LAGGARDS

SEMIS + HARDWARE

Micron, Intel, Lam Research, Dell, Oracle, Western Digital and HPE fell sharply. The concentrated damage matched a duration-sensitive unwind and profit-taking in prior leaders.

03
