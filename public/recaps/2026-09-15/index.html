SEP 15, 2026 · BRENT $108.75 · FOMC PRICES THE CHOICE

THE HIKE IS PRICED.THE REACTION ISN'T.

Brent settled at $108.75, the 10-year Treasury closed at 5.00% and all three major indexes fell for a second session. Markets entered Wednesday with roughly 90% odds of a rate increase. The real question is no longer whether the Fed moves. It is whether the guidance releases the long end, drives it higher, or exposes a credibility shock.

MACRO CONFIRMATION

FIVE-PERCENT YIELDS MET $105 OIL.

Over the full month ending September 15, the 10-year yield moved from 4.72% to 5.00%, the 30-year moved from 5.31% to 5.36%, and front-month WTI moved from $84.50 to $105.83. The 10-year gained 28 basis points while crude rose 25.2%, leaving the Fed decision exposed to both duration and inflation risk.

02

MARKET ROTATION

THE INDEXES FELL. LEADERSHIP DID NOT COLLAPSE.

Exxon gained 2.57%, Chevron 2.64%, AMD 2.19% and Nvidia 0.57%. Microsoft lost 1.64%, Oracle 3.07%, Amazon 2.02% and Alphabet 1.26%. The heatmap shows a market paying for energy exposure and selective chip resilience while cutting duration-sensitive software and discretionary demand.

03

MARKET DRIVERS

THE HIKE IS THE HEADLINE. THE PATH IS THE TRADE.

A damaged Saudi pipeline and pressure on Red Sea shipping pushed Brent higher just as the 10-year held at 5%. A rate increase is largely priced. The open risk is whether Warsh presents it as the start of a cycle, a one-off response to oil, or rejects market pricing with a surprise hold.
