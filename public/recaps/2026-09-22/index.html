SEP 22, 2026 · NASDAQ RECORD · BANKS SLIDE

THE OIL STORY IMPROVED.THE MARKET SPLIT.

The diplomatic path priced on September 21 gained some support. Officials called the latest U.S.-Iran contact productive, another meeting was planned and a possible route toward reopening the Strait of Hormuz took shape. Improving Saudi crude flows also helped. Oil fell below $100, but lower oil did not lift everything. The Nasdaq Composite gained 0.45% to a record, the S&P 500 finished nearly flat and the Dow fell 0.36%. Money stayed in memory and AI while banks sold off.

MACRO CONFIRMATION

OIL EASED. THE MONTHLY PRESSURE REMAINED.

Over the full month ending September 22, the 10-year yield moved from 4.70% to 4.96%, the 30-year moved from 5.23% to 5.29%, and front-month WTI moved from $85.01 to $94.59. Crude backed away from its peak, yet it remained 11.3% higher over the window as both Treasury yields also ended above their starting levels.
