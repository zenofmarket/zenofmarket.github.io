02

MARKET ROTATION

NETWORKING AND POWER WON. STORAGE FELL.

Broadcom, AMD and power-related names advanced while hard-drive, memory and chip-equipment stocks fell. Consumer staples and utilities also gained. This was broader than one stock, but it was not a clean market-wide move.

ROTATION CLASSIFICATIONAI INFRASTRUCTURE WITH A STORAGE SPLIT

Capital stayed inside the AI theme, but moved toward networking, compute and power while storage and memory weakened.

LEADERS

NETWORKING, COMPUTE AND POWER LED

Broadcom rose 3.67%, AMD gained 2.80%, and power-related names were among the strongest groups.

PARTICIPATION

DEFENSIVE GROUPS ALSO HELPED

Walmart, Costco, Procter & Gamble and many utilities added support beyond large technology.

LAGGARDS

STORAGE AND CHIP EQUIPMENT BROKE LOWER

Seagate fell 9.18%, Western Digital lost 6.93%, and several equipment and memory names declined.

THREE FORCES PUSHED THE INDEX THROUGH 7,800.

A breakout forced defensive buyers back into the market. A steady Treasury auction reduced immediate rate pressure. Inside AI, money moved toward networking, compute and power while storage weakened.
