SEP 3, 2026 · YEN JUMPS · RISK ROTATES BROADER

THE HAWKISH TRADE BLINKED.DURATION DIDN'T FULLY CONFIRM.

Small labor and Fed signals triggered an outsized reversal in an extended rate-hike trade. The dollar weakened, the yen surged and all three major U.S. indices gained more than 1%, but the muted duration response leaves the strongest policy-counterforce explanation unproven.

01

MACRO CONFIRMATION

THE UNWIND STARTED BEFORE THE MONTHLY PRESSURE CLEARED.

Over the full month ending September 3, the 10-year yield moved from 4.70% to 4.77%, the 30-year moved from 5.23% to 5.25%, and front-month WTI moved from $80.34 to $91.30. Treasury yields changed little across the month, but crude rose 13.7%, leaving the hawkish unwind without full cross-asset confirmation.

02

MARKET ROTATION

CAPITAL ROTATED. IT DIDN'T RETREAT.

Software, internet platforms and consumer-growth leaders carried the advance, while banks and industrials extended participation. The heatmap validates broad risk-on repair, but weakness in energy and selected semiconductor names keeps the tape rotational rather than indiscriminate.

ROTATION CLASSIFICATIONROTATE INSTEAD OF RETREAT, WITH HIGH INTERNAL DISPERSION.

LEADERSHIP

SOFTWARE + INTERNET

Microsoft, Oracle, Palantir, Alphabet and Meta led rate-sensitive growth, while Tesla and Amazon strengthened the consumer-growth bid.

PARTICIPATION

FINANCIALS JOINED

JPMorgan, Citi, Goldman Sachs and broader capital-markets names advanced, with industrial participation reinforcing the move beyond mega-cap technology.

LAGGARDS

ENERGY + CHIP SPLITS

Energy lagged, Broadcom fell while Nvidia and Intel rose, and healthcare remained mixed. This showed that investors were still discriminating beneath the broad advance.

The hierarchy is clear: extended hawkish positioning amplified the catalyst, TLT determines whether easier conditions are durable, and equity breadth shows how far the unwind transmitted. Mixed macro data keep both policy directions open.

04

CLOSING · NEXT CATALYSTS

THE UNWIND IS REAL. THE POLICY STORY ISN'T PROVEN.

September 3 showed what happens when a policy trade becomes too crowded: modest contradictory signals produced an outsized repricing and broad risk-on repair. The next move depends on whether duration and the next labor report validate easier conditions or expose the rally as a position-clearing event.
