SOFTWARE LEADS · FINANCIALS JOIN · SEMIS STAY SPLIT

CAPITAL ROTATED.
IT DIDN'T RETREAT.

Software, internet platforms and consumer-growth leaders carried the advance, while banks and industrials extended participation. The heatmap validates broad risk-on repair, but weakness in energy and selected semiconductor names keeps the tape rotational rather than indiscriminate.

LEADERSHIP

SOFTWARE + INTERNET

Microsoft, Oracle, Palantir, Alphabet and Meta led rate-sensitive growth, while Tesla and Amazon strengthened the consumer-growth bid.

PARTICIPATION

FINANCIALS JOINED

JPMorgan, Citi, Goldman Sachs and broader capital-markets names advanced, with industrial participation reinforcing the move beyond mega-cap technology.

LAGGARDS

ENERGY + CHIP SPLITS

Energy lagged, Broadcom fell while Nvidia and Intel rose, and healthcare remained mixed. This showed that investors were still discriminating beneath the broad advance.

ROTATION READROTATE INSTEAD OF RETREAT, WITH HIGH INTERNAL DISPERSION.

Leadership migrated from software and mega-cap growth into chips, banks and industrials without broad liquidation elsewhere. That is risk repair, not yet a clean one-way trend.
