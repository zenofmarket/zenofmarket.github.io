SEP 18, 2026 · 5% TREASURY · JAPAN LINK

THE INDEX LOOKED CALM.MOST STOCKS DID NOT.

The S&P 500 gained 0.17% and the Nasdaq Composite rose 0.39%, while the Dow slipped 0.18%. Those small moves hid a weaker session underneath. Most stocks fell, the 10-year Treasury yield returned to 5%, and semiconductor strength kept the indexes steady. The BOJ also raised rates to 1.25%. That matters to the U.S. because Japan is one of the largest foreign holders of Treasuries, linking Japanese policy to future demand for U.S. debt.

MACRO CONFIRMATION

DAILY RELIEF DID NOT UNWIND THE MONTH.

02

MARKET ROTATION

CHIPS HELD THE MARKET UP. MOST GROUPS LAGGED.

Nvidia gained 1.34%, Broadcom 2.97%, AMD 2.70% and Micron 3.92%. Chip equipment was even stronger, with Lam Research up 6.98% and Applied Materials up 6.51%. Software, banks and several consumer names moved the other way. Japan showed a similar split, with AI, memory and weak-yen beneficiaries stronger while software and banks lagged.

03

MARKET DRIVERS

THE U.S. RATE STORY NOW RUNS THROUGH JAPAN.

Japan held about $1.10 trillion of U.S. Treasuries in July, one of the largest reported foreign-country positions. A BOJ hike can change Japanese yields, currency-hedging costs and the appeal of U.S. debt. The next test is whether foreign demand stays firm as Japanese returns become more competitive.
