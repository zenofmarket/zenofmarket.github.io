WEAK JOBS HELPED.THE BIG RISKS STAYED.

Payrolls rose only 29,000, and July and August were revised down by 60,000. Stocks gained as the risk of near-term tightening fell. But long Treasury yields recovered before the close. The jobs report improved the short-term outlook, not the larger risks around inflation, AI funding and future storage supply.

THE DATA

WEAK JOBS EASED POLICY PRESSURE

Payrolls rose 29,000 and unemployment held at 4.2%. Stocks had a clear reason to rally.

THE LIMIT

LONG YIELDS DID NOT STAY DOWN

The bond market still wanted proof that inflation was cooling.

MARKET READ

RELIEF WON THE DAY, NOT THE BIGGER TREND

Stocks kept their gains, but rates and the split inside AI showed that the wider risks remained.

01

MACRO CONFIRMATION

JOBS CHANGED THE NEAR TERM. BONDS DID NOT CONFIRM.

Over the month, the 10-year yield rose from 4.79% to 5.28% and the 30-year rose from 5.27% to 5.63%. WTI moved from $91.00 to $91.27. Weak jobs pulled yields down at first, but the move did not last.

JOINT READSHORT-TERM PRESSURE EASED. LONG YIELDS STAYED HIGH.

The jobs report helped stocks, but CPI must cool before the bond market confirms lasting relief.

02

MARKET ROTATION

AI CHIPS LED. STORAGE FELL.

Big technology, Tesla and Amazon helped the market higher. Nvidia, Broadcom and AMD rose, while Micron, Seagate and Western Digital fell. AI demand stayed strong, but investors did not treat every AI-related company the same.

ROTATION CLASSIFICATIONAI-LED RALLY WITH A SHARP STORAGE SPLIT

Investors favored chip leaders. Storage shares fell because new supply could bring more competition later.

LEADERS

CHIP LEADERS STAYED STRONG

Broadcom gained 3.35%, AMD rose 2.95% and Nvidia added 1.34%.

PARTICIPATION

MEGA-CAPS ALSO ADVANCED

Apple, Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon and Tesla helped broaden the rally.

LAGGARDS

STORAGE PRICED IN FUTURE SUPPLY

Micron lost 2.05%. Seagate and Western Digital fell about 10% even though current storage demand remained firm.

03

MARKET DRIVERS

THREE FORCES SHAPED THE DAY.

Weak jobs helped the market. High long-term yields, large AI funding needs and future storage supply kept the rally selective.
