OCT 5, 2026 · INDEX HIGH · LONG-END STRESS · WEAK BREADTH

Technology lifted the Nasdaq to another high, but the long end of the Treasury market moved the other way. The 10-year closed at 5.31% and the 30-year at 5.66%. Mega-cap strength kept the indexes rising while much of the market remained under pressure.

02

MARKET ROTATION

MEGA-CAPS LED. THE REST OF THE MARKET WAS MIXED.

Nvidia, Broadcom, Microsoft, Alphabet, Meta and Tesla carried the session. Strength also appeared in payments and consumer staples, but semiconductors, healthcare and industrials were uneven. The headline rally was real, yet participation did not match the index high.

ROTATION CLASSIFICATIONMEGA-CAP LED WITH HIGH DISPERSION

Large technology names did most of the work. Gains outside that group were selective rather than broad.

LEADERS

AI AND PLATFORM LEADERS CARRIED THE INDEX

Nvidia rose 2.12%, Broadcom gained 2.08%, Meta added 1.90% and Microsoft advanced 1.48%.

PARTICIPATION

SOME STRENGTH REACHED BEYOND TECHNOLOGY

Visa, Mastercard, Walmart and several consumer staples joined the advance.

LAGGARDS

PRESSURE REMAINED UNDER THE SURFACE

Intel fell 2.63%, Merck lost 3.30%, and parts of healthcare and industrials stayed weak.

03

MARKET DRIVERS

THREE FORCES KEPT THE RALLY UNEVEN.

The front end still reflected softer labor data, but the long end priced a different risk. Concentrated leadership hid weak breadth, while AI demand moved closer to physical limits in power and advanced chip capacity.
