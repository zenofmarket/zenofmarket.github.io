BROAD REBOUND · COMMUNICATIONS AND MATERIALS LEAD

BREADTH IMPROVED.
SOFTWARE DIDN'T JOIN.

Every major sector finished positive or near flat, led by communication services, materials, healthcare and financials. Semiconductors gained on strength in Nvidia and Micron, but sharp losses in software and cybersecurity exposed the session's main fault line.

LEADERSHIP

COMMUNICATIONS + MATERIALS

Meta, Netflix and Alphabet supported communication services, while materials posted the strongest sector-level advance across the map.

PARTICIPATION

FINANCIALS + HEALTHCARE

Visa, Mastercard and major banks joined Johnson & Johnson and UnitedHealth, giving the rebound meaningful participation beyond technology.

LAGGARDS

SOFTWARE + CYBERSECURITY

Microsoft slipped while Palantir and Palo Alto Networks fell sharply. Weakness in Broadcom and AMD also tempered the semiconductor advance.

ROTATION READBROAD RISK REPAIR, WITH A SOFTWARE FAULT LINE.

The heatmap confirms participation across most sectors, but software and cybersecurity show that investors were still discriminating inside growth.

SOFT LABOR CAN SUPPORT RISK · UNTIL IT SIGNALS GROWTH DAMAGE

THE MARKET WANTS COOLING.
NOT A CRACK.

Moderating employment can lower the discount-rate burden and support long-duration assets. The favorable window is narrow: labor must cool enough to restrain yields, but not enough to undermine earnings, demand or credit.
