SEP 23, 2026 · WEAK AUCTION · 10-YEAR AT 5.135%

THE BOND MARKET TOOK CONTROL.AI HELD THE LINE.

The oil and diplomacy relief from the previous two sessions did not disappear. It simply stopped being the market's main focus. Strong business data raised the chance of more Fed tightening, then a weak five-year Treasury auction showed that buyers wanted more yield. The 10-year reached 5.135% and the dollar strengthened. The S&P 500 fell 0.75%, the Dow lost 0.68% and the Nasdaq Composite dropped 1.13%. A few AI and software names held up, but they could not protect the wider market.

MACRO CONFIRMATION

THE BOND SELL-OFF CONFIRMED THE MONTHLY PRESSURE.

Over the full month ending September 23, the 10-year yield moved from 4.70% to 5.11%, the 30-year moved from 5.23% to 5.40%, and front-month WTI moved from $85.01 to $92.16. The 10-year rose 41 basis points and the 30-year 17, overwhelming the smaller retreat in crude from its mid-month peak.

MARKET ROTATION

MOST STOCKS FELL. A FEW POCKETS HELD.

The heatmap was broadly red. Alphabet fell 3.80%, Broadcom lost 2.62%, Amazon dropped 2.24% and Micron slipped 2.21%. Real estate, utilities and consumer names also came under pressure. The exceptions were narrow: Microsoft, Meta, Palantir, cybersecurity and large energy producers finished higher.

03

MARKET DRIVERS

GOOD ECONOMIC NEWS BECAME BAD MARKET NEWS.

September business activity came in much stronger than expected. That raised the chance of more Fed tightening and pushed yields higher. A weak five-year Treasury auction added a second problem: even at high yields, demand was not strong enough. The dollar rose, gold fell and stocks weakened. The bond market set the direction for everything else.
