SEP 1, 2026 · EQUITIES FEEL THE PRESSURE

YIELDS BROKE HIGHER.BOND VOLATILITY DIDN'T.

Sovereign yields remained elevated across major markets, and U.S. equities closed lower with the Nasdaq leading the decline. Selling reached growth and cyclical sectors, but bond volatility stayed contained while energy and healthcare advanced. High rates are reaching risk assets. The unresolved question is whether orderly pressure becomes disorder.

01

MACRO CONFIRMATION

YIELDS AND OIL ROSE TOGETHER.

Over the full month ending September 1, the 10-year yield moved from 4.70% to 4.79%, the 30-year moved from 5.23% to 5.27%, and front-month WTI moved from $80.34 to $90.22. Oil rose 12.3%, far more than either Treasury yield, making energy the clearest source of added inflation pressure.

02

MARKET ROTATION

GROWTH TOOK THE HIT. ENERGY BROKE RANK.

Technology, consumer discretionary, industrials and materials were broadly negative, with software, chips and rate-sensitive cyclicals under pressure. Energy and healthcare moved the other way, while Apple stood out as a rare mega-cap winner.

ROTATION CLASSIFICATIONRISK-OFF WAS BROAD, BUT NOT UNIFORM.

Technology, consumer discretionary, industrials and materials were broadly negative, with software, chips and rate-sensitive cyclicals under pressure. Energy and healthcare moved the other way, while Apple stood out as a rare mega-cap winner.

LEADERSHIP

ENERGY + HEALTHCARE

Exxon Mobil and Chevron led a strong energy pocket, while Johnson & Johnson and UnitedHealth helped healthcare finish in positive territory.

PARTICIPATION

DEFENSIVE POCKETS HELD

Consumer staples added modest support, and Apple's gain made it a notable countertrend outlier inside an otherwise weak growth tape.

LAGGARDS

GROWTH + CYCLICALS

Amazon, Tesla, Nvidia and AMD fell alongside broad weakness in software, industrials and materials, confirming that selling was not confined to one group.

03

THE STORY ISN'T JUST “RATES UP.” IT'S HOW THE MARKET TRANSMITS THEM.

The hierarchy is clear: global yields first, bond volatility second, equity breadth third. Macro data and oil/geopolitics matter mainly through whether they intensify that transmission into broader risk pricing.

04

CLOSING · NEXT CATALYSTS

SEPTEMBER OPENED WITH PRESSURE. NOT YET WITH DISORDER.

The session finished with broad losses across growth and cyclical sectors as elevated yields remained the main macro constraint. The larger September-to-October window can still deliver more volatility. The immediate question is whether defensive resistance holds or a U.S.-specific yield move arrives with volatility confirmation.
