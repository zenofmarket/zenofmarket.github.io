SEP 4, 2026 · INDEX DAMAGE STAYS LIMITED · HARDWARE BREAKS RANK

RATES ROSE.SEMICONDUCTORS REFUSED THE SCRIPT.

A 162,000 increase in August payrolls pushed yields and the dollar higher, yet the broad market refused to behave like a panic tape. The timing raises a harder question: was the market trading the macro shock, or the belief that political pressure would limit the downside? Semiconductors became the cleaner signal by rallying against both.

01

MACRO CONFIRMATION

PAYROLLS LANDED ON A FIRMER RATE AND OIL BACKDROP.

Over the full month ending September 4, the 10-year yield moved from 4.63% to 4.78%, the 30-year moved from 5.18% to 5.24%, and front-month WTI moved from $75.77 to $91.48. The 10-year added 15 basis points and crude rose 20.7%, so the payroll surprise reinforced rather than created the tightening backdrop.

02

MARKET ROTATION

THE INDEX FELL. HARDWARE BROKE RANK.

The heatmap is not a uniform risk-off picture. Semiconductors and computer hardware attracted concentrated buying while software, mega-cap consumer technology and defensives carried much of the red. That split supports a flow-and-visibility rotation rather than a market-wide rejection of growth.

ROTATION CLASSIFICATIONHARDWARE CERTAINTY WON THE DAY, BUT FOLLOW-THROUGH MUST EARN THE STORY.

The heatmap is not a uniform risk-off picture. Semiconductors and computer hardware attracted concentrated buying while software, mega-cap consumer technology and defensives carried much of the red. That split supports a flow-and-visibility rotation rather than a market-wide rejection of growth.

LEADERSHIP

SEMIS + COMPUTER HARDWARE

Micron, AMD, Intel and equipment names posted broad gains, while Dell, Seagate and Western Digital reinforced the hardware bid.

PARTICIPATION

INDUSTRIAL POCKETS HELD

General Electric, Boeing, Caterpillar and selected industrial groups stayed firm, helping keep breadth more balanced than the index decline implied.

LAGGARDS

SOFTWARE + MEGA-CAP DRAG

Microsoft, Alphabet, Apple and Tesla weighed on the tape, while healthcare, staples and energy were broadly soft. The weakness was sizable but far from uniform.
