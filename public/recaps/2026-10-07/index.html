OCT 7, 2026 · GLOBAL YIELD PRESSURE · STRONG AUCTION · NARROW SUPPORT

RATES SHOOK THE MARKET.AI LIMITED THE DAMAGE.

The Dow fell 0.66%, while the S&P 500 and Nasdaq slipped about 0.2%. Long yields climbed sharply before a strong Treasury auction brought them off the highs. AI leaders helped contain the decline, but broad participation stayed weak.

The 10-year yield touched 5.36% before a strong $39 billion auction pulled it back to 5.28%. The 30-year still closed higher at 5.67%. Oil stayed below $90, so the main pressure came from the cost of money.

MARKET ROTATION

THE INDEXES HELD BETTER THAN THE BROAD TAPE.

The Dow fell 0.66%, while the S&P and Nasdaq lost about 0.2%. Large AI names still carried enough weight to limit the headline damage. Smaller companies and the wider market remained under pressure.

ROTATION CLASSIFICATIONNARROW AI-LED DEFENSE

The market did not show broad strength. Concentrated AI leadership kept the main indexes stable while higher rates weighed on the rest.
