25 BP DELIVERED · CONDITIONS NOT RESTRICTIVE · AI HOLDS THE LINE

THE HIKE WAS PRICED.
THE MESSAGE WAS NOT.

The Fed unanimously raised rates to 3.75% to 4.00%, and stocks initially absorbed the move. The break came during the press conference, when persistent inflation and a policy stance not yet clearly restrictive kept more tightening in play. The Dow fell 631 points, while AI hardware left the Nasdaq almost unchanged.
