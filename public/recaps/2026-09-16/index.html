MARKET ROTATION

THE DOW BROKE. AI HELD THE INDEX TOGETHER.

Nvidia rose 0.81%, AMD 1.65% and Intel 4.03%, while JPMorgan lost 1.01%, Bank of America 2.72% and Goldman Sachs 3.96%. Exxon fell 3.54% and ConocoPhillips 6.15% as oil retreated. This was a sharp internal rotation into visible AI earnings, not broad market resilience.
